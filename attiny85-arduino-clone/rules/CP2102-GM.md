# CP2102-GM rules

Source: Silicon Labs, *CP2102/9 Single-Chip USB-to-UART Bridge*, Rev. 1.8:
https://www.silabs.com/documents/public/data-sheets/CP2102-9.pdf

- QFN-28 pin mapping, Table 8 on PDF page 11: D+=4, D-=5, VDD=6,
  REGIN=7, VBUS=8, RST=9, CTS=23, RTS=24, RXD=25, TXD=26, DSR=27,
  DTR=28. The exposed center pad is tied to GND.
- Integrated USB transceiver needs no external USB resistors (front-page
  feature list), so D+/D- are direct.
- Regulator-bypassed self-powered configuration, Figure 9 on PDF page 21:
  feed +3.3 V to VDD and REGIN; use 1.0 µF in parallel with 0.1 µF.
- If VDD/REGIN can be off while VBUS is present, use the Figure 9 VBUS divider:
  47 kΩ high-side and 24 kΩ low-side. This design includes it.
- The datasheet permits an optional 4.7 kΩ RST pull-up for noisy environments;
  R6 implements it.
- CP2102 is UART only. C5 is DNP and AVR ISP remains mandatory for first flash
  and recovery.

## PCB layout and grounding

- The QFN exposed center pad is GND and must be soldered; Table 4 footnote 2
  (PDF page 6) states thermal resistance assumes a multi-layer PCB with any
  exposed pad soldered to a PCB pad. PCB-stage rule: tie the exposed pad to the
  GND pour with a via cluster under/around the pad.
- The datasheet publishes no trace-impedance requirement; CP2102 is USB
  full-speed (12 Mbps), so keep D+/D- short, paired, and away from switching
  nodes rather than impedance-controlled.
